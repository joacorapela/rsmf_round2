While most current ML methods are designed for offline, disk-based processing, Bonsai uniquely requires real-time, online processing within closed-loop architectures. This shift from post-hoc analysis to live experimental control has two transformative implications:

\begin{enumerate}

    \item \textbf{For ML Developers:} As a robust source of real-time neural and behavioral data, \textbf{Bonsai} serves as an ideal platform for developers seeking to apply and validate algorithms in live, dynamic environments.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent \textbf{Benefits to the UK Research Landscape}

The proposed documentation and dissemination activities will empower the UK
research community to integrate sophisticated Machine Learning (ML) into
\textbf{Bonsai} workflows, driving unprecedented neuroscientific findings.
%
The defining achievement of Bonsai’s reactive visual programming language is
its ability to \textbf{collapse the gap between conceptual design and
experimental execution.} It enables domain experts to rapidly architect and
deploy high-complexity, multi-device experiments—tasks that would typically
require a dedicated programmer days or weeks of development, if they were
achievable at all.

While most current ML frameworks are designed for offline, disk-based
processing, \textbf{Bonsai.ML} uniquely enables \textbf{real-time, online
processing} within closed-loop architectures. This transition from post-hoc
analysis to live experimental control has two transformative implications for
UK research:

\begin{enumerate}

    \item \textbf{For Neuroscientists:} It provides a novel toolkit for
        real-time data interaction, enabling the discovery of brain functions
        observable only during active, closed-loop manipulation.

    \item \textbf{For ML Developers:} By providing a robust stream of real-time
        neural and behavioral data, \textbf{Bonsai} serves as a premier
        platform for developers to validate and deploy algorithms in dynamic,
        live environments.

\end{enumerate}

We have focused on applications of Bonsai.ML to experimental neuroscience,
because this is our research area of expertise. However, Bonsai.ML should be

Ultimately, the impact of this project extends far beyond experimental control
