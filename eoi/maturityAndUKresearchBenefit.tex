Bonsai is used by multiple research laboratories at many UK institutions, like
UCL, Francis Crick Institute and MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology.
%
It was created in 2012 and has more than 28 public releases.  Please refer to
the \href{https://github.com/bonsai-rx/bonsai/releases}{releases listing in
GitHub}.

Bonsai allows to create issues and provide feedback through the Issues and
Discussion sections of its \href{https://github.com/bonsai-rx/bonsai}{GitHub
repository}.
%
Contributions can be made by opening pull requests, as indicated in its
\href{https://bonsai-rx.org/contribute/}{Contribution Guide}. In addition,
Bonsai has its own package manager and most often Bonsai users contribute to
the ecosystem by creating Bonsai packages, as explained in the
\href{https://bonsai-rx.org/docs/articles/create-package.html}{documentation}.
